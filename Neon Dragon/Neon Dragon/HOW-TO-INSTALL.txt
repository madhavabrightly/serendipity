Neon Dragon - Windows Cursor Pack
=================================

HOW TO INSTALL (takes about 1 minute):

  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop).

  2. Right-click the file "install.inf" and choose "Install".
     (On Windows 11, click "Show more options" first if you
     don't see Install.)

  3. Open Windows cursor settings:
     Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse >
     "Additional mouse settings" > "Pointers" tab.

  4. In the "Scheme" dropdown pick "Neon Dragon",
     then click "Apply". Done - enjoy your new cursors!

NOTE: Installing does not change your cursor automatically -
step 3 and 4 are needed to actually switch to the new scheme.

TO GO BACK: open the same Pointers tab and pick
"Windows Default" in the Scheme dropdown, then Apply.
